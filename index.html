DTR Calculator

Office: standard 8:30 to 5:30 with 1 hour lunch. Late and undertime are counted, early in and overtime are not. A late day still counts as present, and a half day counts as 0.5. Minutes late typed on an Office day replace the time in. Diner and Long shift: every 12 hours is 1 day plus 4 hours OT. Absent and Holiday are counted separately. Night diff is 10PM to 6AM. If time out is earlier than time in, it counts as next day.
